## 11. What would change our mind

P crosses 0.50 (PREREG 8) when the required growth k* falls to 0.187x or below. On its own that needs a capture rate of 8.439 of gross fees (above 1, impossible), or issuance cut by 96.8 percent, or a NEAR price at 0.032x today's. Single-factor rows of the registered grid: G_P = 0.5: P = 0.083; G_P = 2.0: P = 0.028; issuance = half: P = 0.083; capture = 0.34: P = 0.065; capture = 0.5: P = 0.076. None crosses 0.50.

The data would change our mind if coverage C_90 rises toward 1 on the daily snapshots (`history.csv`), if a later backtest with more origin years shows the class frequency under-forecasting growth, or if the registered forecasts resolve against us on 2027-11-11, 2028-11-11, 2029-11-11 (`RESOLUTION.md`).

## 12. What this cannot tell you

- No price forecast. Price enters only as a scenario factor G_P (0.5, 1, 2).
- Frequencies are not mechanisms. A base rate says how often fee growth of this size happened to comparable protocols, not why, and not whether NEAR Intents is like them.
- DefiLlama lists what it lists. Protocols it does not track, or tracks late, are absent; the panel is what survived to be listed.
- "Fees" differ across protocols: each DefiLlama adapter defines its own fees.
- Capture and issuance are governance decisions. G_kappa and G_I are scenarios, not forecasts.
- Aggregator data, not an on-chain rebuild: buybacks are DefiLlama holders revenue, supply and price are NearBlocks charts.
- Age is the age in DefiLlama's data (days since the first day with fees > 0 in the panel), not the age since launch.
- Dead fill uses the panel end D: an entity silent for more than 30 days before D is filled with zeros, so a backtest pool outcome can use knowledge of D rather than of its tau.
- Class R selects protocols at a high point of their fees (scale band around NEAR Intents' current fees), so part of the typical decline in the class is regression to the mean.
- Few independent market cycles: the class R origins at h = 24 run from 2020 to 2024, and one market regime can dominate the class.
- Class R mixes fee types (trading fees, interest, staking yield). Restricted to trading-type categories (R_trading, 38 entities) the primary probability is 0.049 (0.009 to 0.098), against 0.053 for R.

**2026-11-13: judgement calls of the third pass (not changes of method)**

(a) "Improve the held-out scores". `PREREG_3.md` T2 and `docs/TASKS_V3.md` W4 do not define
when recalibration improves the held-out scores. The rule used (`recalibration.improves`, fixed
before the thesis text was written and stated in `THESIS.md` section 8): the mean Brier score is
lower for the three events (fees maintained, doubled, at least 5x), no 90 percent interval of the

below 0, so P_cal is quoted, always after the registered probability.

(b) T4 not reported. `PREREG_3.md` T4 says to report the failure instead of a number if the fit

domain) 116 of 2000 resamples (5.8 percent) did not converge; across the definitions tried
(an optimum on the edge of the initial grid, or on a bound, with 21- or 41-point grids) the
share ranged from 2.5 to 5.8 percent, around the stop line. Because the definition was not
registered and the outcome depends on it, no T4 number is reported. The point fit seen during
these runs (0.047 against the empirical 0.053) is recorded in `PROGRESS.md` only and is stated as
unreported in `THESIS.md`.

protocols). The bootstrap interval stays reported next to them, as registered.
