## 1. The claim

**Buybacks are unlikely to cover issuance at the 24-month mark.** The pre-registered probability that buybacks funded by NEAR Intents revenue cover NEAR's net issuance 24 months after 2026-11-11 (primary scenario: capture, price and issuance rate unchanged) is **0.053** (90% interval 0.022 to 0.091, cluster bootstrap over entities, 90 percent), from model B0 on class R (75 entities), snapshot 2026-11-11.

## 2. Evidence for

- Coverage today is C_90 = 0.182: annualised buybacks of $20.8M against net issuance of 31,766,510 NEAR per year (r = 2.48 percent) at $3.58. With capture, price and issuance rate unchanged, 90-day fees must grow by k* = 5.76x in 24 months.
- In class R (75 entities, 525 moments) the share of 24-month outcomes with fee growth >= k* is 0.053 (90% interval 0.022 to 0.091). Using only each entity's earliest moment: 6 of 75 = 0.080 (Clopper-Pearson 0.035 to 0.152).
- The analog model (nearest entities on scale, momentum and age) gives A_30 = 0.000 (0.000 to 0.095), A_60 = 0.017 (0.001 to 0.077), A_120 = 0.025 (0.007 to 0.063).
- Across the 864 rows of the specification curve, P has median 0.074, minimum 0.000 and maximum 0.294.
- Moving P across 0.50 alone would need capture G_kappa >= 30.87 (a capture rate of 8.44), or issuance cut to 0.034x today's path, or a price factor <= 0.032. No single-factor row of the grid crosses 0.50.
- In the 12-month rolling-origin backtest the class frequency B0 is not beaten by the analog model and scores better than both trend baselines (section 6).

## 3. Evidence against

- NEAR Intents fees are 1.83x their level of six months earlier: A_90(f) = $75.9M per year at t0 against $41.5M for the 90 days ending 2026-05-11.
- Coverage by window: 30 days 0.193, 90 days 0.182, 180 days 0.122, since 2026-02-23 0.099; capture rate kappa_30 = 0.312, kappa_90 = 0.273.
- No 24-month backtest: all 335 candidate targets were dropped, so the 24-month model is not tested out of sample.
- Every scored 12-month target has its origin in 2025: the backtest covers one calendar year.
- Capture and issuance are governance decisions and can change faster than fees.

## 4. What would change our mind

P crosses 0.50 (PREREG 8) when k* falls to 0.187 or below. On its own that needs one of:

- capture: G_kappa >= 30.87, a capture rate of 8.439 of gross fees (above 1, so capture alone cannot do it);
- issuance: G_I <= 0.034, a cut of 96.8 percent from the constant-rate path;
- price: G_P <= 0.032, i.e. NEAR at 0.032x today's price with everything else unchanged.

Single-factor rows of the registered grid (primary class, W = 90, h = 24): G_P = 0.5: P = 0.083; G_P = 2.0: P = 0.028; issuance = half: P = 0.083; capture = 0.34: P = 0.065; capture = 0.5: P = 0.076. None of them crosses 0.50.

We would also change our mind if a sufficient 24-month backtest showed the class frequency to be miscalibrated for protocols like NEAR Intents.

## 8. What this cannot tell you

- No price forecast. Price enters only as a scenario factor G_P (0.5, 1, 2).
- Frequencies are not mechanisms. A base rate says how often fee growth of this size happened to comparable protocols, not why, and not whether NEAR Intents is like them.
- DefiLlama lists what it lists. Protocols it does not track, or tracks late, are absent; the panel is what survived to be listed.
- "Fees" differ across protocols: each DefiLlama adapter defines its own fees.
- Capture and issuance are governance decisions. G_kappa and G_I are scenarios, not forecasts.
- Aggregator data, not an on-chain rebuild: buybacks are DefiLlama holders revenue, supply and price are NearBlocks charts.
- Age is the age in DefiLlama's data (days since the first day with fees > 0 in the panel), not the age since launch.
- Dead fill uses the panel end D: an entity silent for more than 30 days before D is filled with zeros, so a backtest pool outcome can use knowledge of D rather than of its tau.
- Class R selects protocols at a high point of their fees (scale band around NEAR Intents' current fees), so part of the typical decline in the class is regression to the mean.
- Few independent market cycles: the class R origins at h = 24 run from 2020 to 2024, and one market regime can dominate the class.
- Class R mixes fee types (trading fees, interest, staking yield). Restricted to trading-type categories (R_trading, 38 entities) the primary probability is 0.049 (0.009 to 0.098), against 0.053 for R.
